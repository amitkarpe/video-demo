# Engineering and audiovisual review

- **Source fidelity:** current source matched pinned baseline `5f815709f846b868248d681a4c0016fa2f85aae5`. Reviewed the executable grouping, verdict, scoring, diff, accuracy and audit predicates as well as recorded metrics. The film distinguishes observation, deterministic transformation, model proposal and human judgment. The verdict docstring mismatch is explicitly shown.
- **Failure cases:** unknown evidence rejection, unscored/needs-human fallback, WebGoat false suppression under unchanged precision, missing-scanner `not_retested`, and hash-chain completeness/anchor limits are explained. Illustrative cases are not represented as new scanner runs or real remediation.
- **Media:** FFprobe confirms 533 seconds, 1920×1080 H.264/AAC; the entire final export decoded with FFmpeg. Caption timing comes from 88 individually synthesized sentences with zero overlapping cues, rather than interpolating sentences within long audio files.
- **Visual review:** a 12-second WebGoat proof was rendered and inspected before full production. Sampled each chapter and inspected the false-suppression sequence at 1280×720 as a laptop-size proxy. Fixed opening-card overlap, a crowded proof label, the legend/caption collision and the simplified predicate. Animation events for field mapping, admission and suppression are tied to the corresponding narration cues. This is a restrained diagrammatic treatment; perceived polish and comprehension are not audience-tested.
- **Automated composition checks:** runtime, motion and audio checks passed; contrast 139/139. The tool warns that some secondary labels are below its 28px type floor and reports intentionally off-canvas panels. Those limitations remain visible in the source; no universal readability claim is made.
- **Voice:** synthetic local Kokoro male voice. Programmatic timing/decoding does not establish correct pronunciation or good prosody. No phonetic substitutions were applied to on-screen terms; OWASP, CORS, CVSS, Nikto, Nuclei and crAPI need listener review.
- **Reproducibility and cost:** sentence audio and editable sources are retained; content hashes allow unchanged speech to be reused. No paid API/service, live scan, AWS operation, source-repository mutation or public deployment was used. First renderer cancellation exposed a delayed shutdown/lock; the final render was allowed to complete normally.

The engineering conclusion is bounded: these records make grouping, model proposals, filtering and changes inspectable. They do not prove exploitability, comprehensive recall, safe suppression or analyst-hours saved. The next evaluation is expert ground-truth review, false-suppression analysis, grouping review and a measured manual baseline. This production is not evidence of client engagement readiness.
