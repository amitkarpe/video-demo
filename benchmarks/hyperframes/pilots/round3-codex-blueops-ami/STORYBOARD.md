# BlueOps — The Trust Factory

An original, illustrated factory film for issue #24. The opening asks why a green build is not enough. A golden image moves through one shared foundation, three workload stations, proof gates, a human decision, a conditional future schedule, and a visible route back.

## Visual and editorial rationale

The warm workshop represents construction. Teal conveys evidence, amber conveys conditions, coral conveys failure, and violet distinguishes the database family. The night version of the same generated set represents a future maintenance window. These are fictional visual metaphors, not screenshots of live systems.

The camera enters the factory, then reveals a schematic working floor. Original SVG machines inherit the generated set's silhouettes and colors. Drawn paths, traveling packets, task placement, a written approval, and a reversing image make causality visible. Labels identify current evidence, required acceptance, proposed gates, and unknown states. The film does not treat a passing animation as operational evidence.

## Sound and captions

## Iterations

1. Research and script: reconcile the public requested story with current source boundaries. Separate requirements, limited current evidence, proposed tests and future policy.
2. Artwork: generate the warm factory; edit the same image into the night setting to preserve geometry.
3. Composition: build original machines and motion; review browser scenes and a full encoded draft.
4. Caption/motion revision: remove orphan one-word and very short final phrases; strengthen the disclosure backing; preserve proposed dashed edges; animate three ECS packets before the demo pass. A final requirements pass also makes ECS-agent health explicit. Final frame and playback review follows the encoded draft.

No winning score is assigned. Human listening, pacing preference and creative acceptance remain reviewer decisions.
