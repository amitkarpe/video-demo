Who allowed this fix, and can we prove it was fixed?

Defense Factory is a calm page for the cloud security work Ops already does. It reads a notice or a scanner finding, finds the servers it touches, says what to patch first, and leaves a proof after the fix.

The problem

  • Notices and scanner files arrive every week.
  • Which servers? Which first? Who said yes? Did it work?
  • The answers live in mail, chat and memory.

The idea

  • Read the notice. Find servers and images.
  • Give each patch a call: Patch now, Plan or Defer.
  • Fix with one approval, check again, keep the proof.

The rule

The page advises and a person decides. It never says "safe", "reachable" or "exploitable" unless it proved it.

5 · Limits

What it is, and what is not proven yet

What it is today

  • A lab account, on a personal Mac. Never PROD, never an office account.
  • Calls are advice: EPSS is a prediction, not proof for this server.
  • Network exposure is not assessed. A deferred row stays an open finding.
  • Every change waits for a person's Approve.
Not proven yet
  • OPENInspector after-count of rebuilt images.
  • OPENNo real "no fix available" finding has gone through the page.
  • OPENThe Defer button was not pressed on a real row.
  • OPEN"Where it listens" shows local sockets, not reachability.
  • OPENNo real receipt from the PF factory has been read yet.
  • OPENThe AI reader with a direct provider is not proven in the lab.
