Every week, a new security warning reaches us.
A flaw is found in a software package. Which of our servers have it?
Any team can drop it in: an email, a spreadsheet, a list, or a short note.
Our agents pick it up. They run on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
It is an AWS service that other departments already use for their agents.
The Harness runs each agent's work, and the Runtime gives every session its own sealed space.
Identity gives each agent its own identity and keys, so it reaches only what it is allowed.
Gateway puts our tools behind one gate, and Policy checks every call against our rules before it runs.
Observability records every step in Amazon CloudWatch.
The agents read our AWS environment without changing it, and find every server with that package.
Then they prepare the exact fix: one reviewed patch for those servers, not a free command.
Nothing changes until a person decides. One approval, for this one change.
The patch goes out. Then we check again, separately, and the proof goes to audit.
This is our vision: every cloud operation, done this way.
Routine work runs on its own. Security keeps a person in the loop.
Defence Factory. Agents prepare. People decide. The proof stays.
