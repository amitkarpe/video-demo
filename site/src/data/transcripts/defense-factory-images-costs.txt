Each month we make a fresh, fully patched server image. And we tidy up what still costs money.
After the weekly patching, one click makes this month's image: a fully patched copy of a server.
One approval replaces the servers with new ones from that image. One at a time, with a check after each.
If the new image is wrong, one click takes the servers back to the image before.
The cost check lists what costs money for nothing: unused disks, snapshots, stopped servers. A check changes nothing.
Only this project's own leftovers can be deleted here, and only after an approval. The rest goes on a list for its owner.
The page shows both leftovers deleted, and the saving each month. The proof stays.
