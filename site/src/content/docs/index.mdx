## How these films are made

Each film is code: a HyperFrames composition (HTML, GSAP and Three.js), an AI narration voice that is disclosed on screen, a synthesized or credited music bed, and burned-in captions. Every statement is checked against the source repository before release. Films are made in a private factory and published here only when the owner approves them. Videos stream from this repository's GitHub Releases.
